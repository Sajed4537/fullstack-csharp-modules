# Module 01 — OOP (Object-Oriented Programming)

## Summary
Fondamentaux de la programmation orientée objet en C# : classes & objets, encapsulation, héritage, polymorphisme, interfaces, classes abstraites et exceptions.

## Exercises
- `ex01-classes-and-objects` — Classes & Objects
- `ex02-encapsulation-and-validation` — Encapsulation & Validation
- `ex03-inheritance-and-polymorphism` — Inheritance & Polymorphism
- `ex04-interfaces-and-abstract-classes` — Interfaces & Abstract Classes
- `ex05-advanced-polymorphism-and-collections` — Advanced Polymorphism & Collections
- `ex06-exceptions-and-validation` — Exceptions & Validation
